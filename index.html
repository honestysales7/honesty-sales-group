Engineering services

Landfill construction quality assurance & field engineering

We provide CQA engineers and field supervision for landfill operators, contractors and engineering consultancies. On site, we check that every layer and installation is built to the approved design and CQA plan, and we keep the records and test evidence that the CQA validation report depends on.

Cell construction CQA

Full-time supervision of new cell construction, so that each stage is inspected, tested and signed off before the next layer goes on and before waste is placed.

  • Formation and subgrade preparation
  • Engineered clay and artificially established geological barriers
  • Geomembrane and geosynthetic lining systems
  • Protection layers, leachate drainage blankets and collection pipework

Capping & restoration

CQA supervision of capping on completed landfill phases, sealing waste in place to control rainwater ingress, leachate generation and gas emissions.

  • Regulating and profiling layers
  • Clay and geosynthetic cap barriers
  • Cap drainage and restoration soils
  • Thickness, level and profile checks

Gas well drilling supervision

Supervising the drilling and installation of landfill gas extraction wells, so that wells are built to specification and recorded accurately.

  • Drilling into waste and logging arisings
  • Well depth, casing and slotted screen checks
  • Gravel pack, seals and wellhead installation
  • As-built well records

Leachate well drilling supervision

Supervising leachate extraction and monitoring wells, which let operators control leachate levels and protect the lining system.

  • Borehole drilling and logging
  • Well construction against design
  • Installation and completion records
  • Safe working when drilling into waste

Testing, records & reporting

Clear, auditable records from the field, ready for the CQA validation report and for the regulator.

  • Daily site diaries and photographic records
  • Material sampling and in-situ testing
  • Non-conformance reporting and close-out
  • Inputs to CQA validation reports
